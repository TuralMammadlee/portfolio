01 / about

About me

I'm a master's student in Computer Science at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. My focus areas are Human-Centered Machine Intelligence, and Software Engineering and Compiler Construction. I did my bachelor's at Riga Technical University.

My work sits between machine learning and software engineering. I have built medical imaging pipelines, fine-tuned and served LLMs, and written research code that other people have to run. I like measuring things. In the merge-conflict project I benchmarked 10 open LLMs and found that model confidence barely separates right answers from wrong ones (AUROC 0.62).

I now split my time between research at KIT and a production system for the start-up support unit, so I spend a lot of it on tests, architecture decisions and reproducible experiments.

Scalable checkers engine

A checkers engine for boards from 6×6 to 18×18 that pairs alpha-beta minimax with a residual CNN evaluator trained through self-play. The hybrid evaluation reached a 63.5% win rate over 500 games against a 51.0% baseline, and won 12 of 20 games against the Raven engine.

Evolve an image in your browser

This is the idea behind my Image-approximation repository, rewritten in JavaScript. A population of 80 random 20×20 black-and-white images competes to match the target. Each generation keeps the two best, picks parents by tournament, mixes them pixel by pixel and flips a few pixels at random.
